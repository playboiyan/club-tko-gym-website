About CLUB TKO Gym

CLUB TKO Gym is a fitness centre located in Braamfontein, Johannesburg. The gym provides affordable fitness facilities, modern strength and cardio equipment, group fitness and personal training services.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, good quality fitness services and professional support that help members improve their health, confidence and overall physicality.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of Johannesburg's leading fitness centres by creating a welcoming environment where people can achieve their health and fitness goals.

Our trainers provide guidance and support to help members work towards their fitness goals.
